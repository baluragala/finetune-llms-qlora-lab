Checkpoint 1 · Capability vs disposition

A raw, pretrained-only model is asked: “Write me a haiku about the sea.” Why might it not write one, and what does that reveal about pretraining?

Model answer

Pretraining only taught it to continue plausible text, not to follow instructions. Online, that line could be followed by anything. The model has the capability to write a haiku but not the disposition to treat your sentence as a command. Closing that gap is exactly what instruction tuning does.

Let the room attempt it before you click. The point: capability is not disposition. Tie it back to the base-vs-instruct slide: the base model rambled because nobody taught it that a ticket means make this now.
