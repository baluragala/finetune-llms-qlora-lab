Instruction tuning and SFT

Why

A base model has capability but not obedience: it continues text instead of following orders.

What

Supervised fine-tuning on (instruction → ideal response) pairs. SFT is the mechanism; instruction tuning is SFT aimed at following instructions in general.

How

Format each pair in the model’s chat template, train it to predict the response, and measure on held-out pairs. In the lab: 281 (ticket → triage JSON) pairs.

SFT is what you teach; LoRA or QLoRA is how you teach it cheaply. Not either/or.

Teach the chef that a ticket is a command: ticket plus the correct finished plate, repeated until “make exactly that” is second nature.

SFT is the mechanism; instruction tuning teaches obedience. You usually do SFT via LoRA, not instead of it. The lab is SFT on 281 ticket-to-JSON pairs, delivered through QLoRA.
