Lab · Why QLoRA fits a free GPU

Training memory is mostly not the weights

For a 1.5B modelFull fine-tune (fp16)QLoRA (this lab)
Base weights~3 GB~1 GB (4-bit)
Gradients + optimizer state~12 GB+tens of MB (LoRA only)
Activationshighlower (gradient checkpointing)
Fits a 15 GB T4?No, too tightYes, about 6–8 GB

4-bit base: ~4× smaller

Frozen: no optimizer state for it

LoRA: gradients for ~2% of weights

Checkpointing: recompute, don’t store

Adam keeps two extra numbers per weight, so optimizer state dominates training memory. QLoRA attacks every row of the table. Our 0.5B default has even more headroom; the 1.5B numbers show you can scale up on the same free GPU. On a T4 use fp16; on Ampere or newer GPUs use bf16.
