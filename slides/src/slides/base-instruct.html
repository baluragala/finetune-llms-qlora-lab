2 · Base vs instruct

Same weights at birth, different habits

Same prompt to both: “Classify this ticket and reply with ONLY JSON like {"category": "..."}. Ticket: I was charged twice for my Pro plan.”

Base · Qwen2.5-0.5B

Brilliant autocomplete

Trained only to predict the next token. It ignores the instruction, repeats or continues the text, and doesn’t stop.

Instruct · Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct

Follows the ticket

Further trained on instruction → response pairs. Returns clean JSON, but with its own label: "Billing and Service Issues", not our "Billing".

Instruction tuning gives format-following for free. It does not know your labels, scale or schema. That last gap is the job fine-tuning does.

This is the live demo from the lab notebook, section 1c. Base models ramble; instruct models obey but use their own vocabulary. That is why we always fine-tune starting from an instruct model, and why we still need to fine-tune at all.
