Chef Qwen and the Giant Recipe Book

The premise

Chef Qwen can cook almost anything, thanks to a Giant Recipe Book with half a billion tiny notes in it. You run the Help Desk Café and need one special dish, every time: a card that says which team · how urgent · a kind reply.

The problem

Chef Qwen serves “Billing and Service Issues!” Your card only has a box called “Billing”. The chef is clever but doesn’t know your café’s rules. How do you teach them?

Idea 1: rewrite the whole book (full fine-tuning)

You’d need a library-sized room, it would take ages, and while rewriting you might rub out recipes the chef already knew. Far too much work to teach one dish.

Tell it as a story; the room will remember it longer than any formula. Map as you go: the book is the model's weights (about 500 million numbers), the special dish is our triage JSON, rewriting the book is full fine-tuning, and rubbing out old recipes is catastrophic forgetting. Ask: "Would you reprint a famous chef's whole cookbook to teach one dish?" Let them say no, then turn the slide.
