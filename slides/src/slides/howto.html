How this deck works

Every concept answers three questions

Why

The problem it solves

What goes wrong without it. If you can’t say why, you won’t know when to use it.

What

The idea in plain words

The concept, the moving parts, and the vocabulary you need, without the maths first.

How

How you use it

The decision rule, the setting, or the code. In the lab, exactly what to run and what to look for.

A shaded strip like this one is the kitchen analogy: the same idea, told as a chef’s apprenticeship.

Dark Checkpoint slides: answer first, then click to reveal the model answer.

Tell the room how to read the deck. Why / What / How on every concept slide; the analogy strip ties it back to the chef; checkpoints are for the room to answer before you click. Encourage people to commit to an answer out loud before the reveal.
