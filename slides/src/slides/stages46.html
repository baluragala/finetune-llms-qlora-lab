4 · Stages 4–6

Approach, train, evaluate

4

Choose the approach

Options differ in cost by orders of magnitude. Most teams should start with LoRA or QLoRA.

5

Training

The dials are learning rate and epochs. Train conservatively. Validation loss rising while training loss falls means memorization: stop.

6

Evaluation

A held-out set and a real baseline (the prompted base model). Is it better than the cheap option, by enough to justify the cost? Plus a regression check.

In the lab: QLoRA on a T4 · loss 2.14 → 0.12, eval loss falling every epoch · baseline measured before training, then the same test re-run after.

Start with LoRA or QLoRA. Train conservatively. The overfitting signal is validation loss rising while training loss keeps falling. Evaluation always needs a baseline, and the cheapest honest baseline is the prompted base model.
