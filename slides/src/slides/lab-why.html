Lab · Why fine-tune here

Our decision questions, applied

ApproachVerdict for ticket triage
Better promptTried: a 0.5B model follows long instructions unreliably. Category accuracy 23–31%. And every call carries the long prompt.
RAGAdds knowledge. Our gap is behaviour (format and labelling policy). There’s nothing to look up.
Big hosted modelWorks, but costs per ticket, adds latency, and sends customer data to a third party.
Fine-tune a small modelBakes format and policy into the weights: short prompts, fast, cheap, private, consistent.

Prompting and RAG change what the model knows at request time. Fine-tuning changes how it behaves every time. This is a behaviour problem.
