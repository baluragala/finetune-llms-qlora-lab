Checkpoint 2 · Facts vs behaviour

The support bot keeps citing last year’s refund policy. Someone proposes fine-tuning it on the new policy document. Good idea? What would you do instead?

Model answer

Bad idea. A policy is knowledge that changes: fine-tuning’s worst case. You’d pay to hard-code a fact that goes stale on the next legal update, and the model may still mix up old and new. Use RAG: store the current policy, fetch the relevant section at answer time. A policy change becomes a one-document edit, not a retrain. Save fine-tuning for how the bot answers: tone, structure, refusals.

A classic trap. The policy is changing knowledge, so the answer is RAG. Contrast with the lab: our categories and JSON format are behaviour, which is why fine-tuning fits there.
