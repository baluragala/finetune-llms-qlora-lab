Preference tuning: RLHF and DPO

Of two correct answers, one is more helpful, safer or on-tone. Preference tuning teaches that taste: most of what we call alignment.

RLHF

Train a judge, then optimize

People rank pairs of answers → train a reward model → use reinforcement learning to make the LLM score high. Powerful, multi-stage, somewhat unstable.

DPO

Learn straight from the pairs

The same “A is better than B” data, but optimize the model directly: no reward model, no RL. Simpler, more stable, very popular.

Refine the palate: the head chef picks the better of two good plates, thousands of times.

Between two good answers, which is better? Preference tuning teaches taste and alignment. RLHF is reward model plus RL: powerful, fiddly. DPO learns directly from preference pairs: simpler and now the common choice.
