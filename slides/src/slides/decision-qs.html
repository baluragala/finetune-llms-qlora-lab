3 · Ask in order, stop at the first yes

Five questions before you fine-tune

1. Can a better prompt fix it? It’s free and fast. Always try it first.

2. Is knowledge missing or changing? Use RAG. Never fine-tune facts that change.

3. Do you need consistent behaviour, style, format or skill, and have examples of “right”? Fine-tune. This is its home turf.

4. Is it a fixed task at high volume where cost or latency hurts? Fine-tune a smaller model.

5. More than one of the above? Combine: fine-tune for voice and format, RAG for facts.

Stop at the first yes. Question 1 honestly kills most fine-tuning requests. Our lab problem passes question 3: consistent labels and a strict format, with clear right answers.
