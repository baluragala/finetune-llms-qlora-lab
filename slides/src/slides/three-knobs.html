3 · The core distinction

Three knobs, three different jobs

Fine-tuning

Changes how the model behaves

RAG

Changes what the model knows right now

Prompting

Changes what you ask in this moment

Not competitors: a sommelier’s trained taste (fine-tuning) plus tonight’s wine list (RAG) plus the guest’s request (prompt).

Use the cheapest tool that solves the problem, and combine them when you need to.

This is the single most important distinction of the day. Say it as a rule and come back to it: behaviour, knowledge, request. Most production systems combine all three.
