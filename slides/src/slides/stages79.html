4 · Stages 7–9

Deploy, monitor, iterate

7

Deployment

Behind a flag, rolled out gradually, with instant rollback. LoRA adapters are small and hot-swappable, so rollback is “detach the adapter”.

8

Monitoring and drift

Live quality, user signals, distribution drift. A model frozen in time quietly decays as the world moves.

9

Iteration

It’s a loop. Failure cases are your highest-quality new training data: add them, retrain, re-evaluate, redeploy.

In the lab: we save both a ~35 MB adapter and a merged ~1 GB model, and the misclassified tickets show exactly what data to add next.

Ship behind a flag and roll back fast; LoRA shines here. Monitor for drift. Failures are your best future training data. In the lab the priority misses on Urgent tickets are precisely that.
