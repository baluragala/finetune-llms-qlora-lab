5 · Taxonomy · A

Full fine-tuning

Why

Maximum control. For deep changes of domain, language or behaviour, when you have lots of data and budget.

What

Update every weight: all billions are free to change.

How to think about it

Needs a big dataset and GPU memory of many times the model size (weights + gradients + optimizer state), plus a full model copy per task. Highest risk of catastrophic forgetting.

Most teams don’t need this. Reach for it only when LoRA has clearly hit its ceiling.

Retrain every instinct the chef has, from knife grip to plating. Total transformation, total cost.

All weights move. Most powerful, most expensive, highest forgetting risk. Reserve for deep domain shifts with ample data and budget. In the lab's memory table, full fine-tuning of even a 1.5B model doesn't fit a free T4.
