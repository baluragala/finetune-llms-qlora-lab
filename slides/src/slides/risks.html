6 · Key risks and mitigations

Four ways fine-tuning quietly hurts you

RiskWhat happensMitigation
Catastrophic forgettingThe narrow task overwrites general skillsLoRA (frozen base), low learning rate and few epochs, mix in general data, run a regression test
OverfittingMemorizes examples; brittle on new onesStop when validation loss rises, add diversity, prefer LoRA
Data leakageTest examples bleed into training; personal data gets memorizedStrict de-duplicated splits, scrub PII, test for regurgitation
Alignment regressionSafety and tone guardrails erodeMix in safety data, red-team before and after, gate the release on it

Freezing the base with LoRA mitigates several of these at once. That is a structural choice, not a training trick.

Forgetting, overfitting, leakage, alignment regression, each with a structural mitigation. Kitchen versions: the pastry obsessive who forgot how to grill; the cook who freezes on the eleventh plate; practising only the exam dish; dropping hygiene for speed.
