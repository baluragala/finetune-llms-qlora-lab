Checkpoint 6 · Synthesis

In one line each: when do you reach for prompting, RAG and fine-tuning? And what’s the one phrase for the whole decision?

Model answer

Prompting when a better instruction can steer what the model already does. RAG when the gap is missing or changing knowledge at answer time. Fine-tuning when you need consistent behaviour, style, format or skill baked in, with clean examples and an honest evaluation.

“Fine-tune the craft, retrieve the facts, prompt the moment, and use the cheapest tool that solves the problem.”

The synthesis check. End on the one phrase, and have the room say it back.
