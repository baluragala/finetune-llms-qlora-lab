5 · Taxonomy · The map

Two questions sort every technique

Question 1 · How many weights move?

The cost axis

Full fine-tuning: every weight. LoRA: small add-on matrices, base frozen. QLoRA: LoRA on a 4-bit compressed base, the cheapest of all.

Question 2 · What signal teaches it?

The goal axis

SFT: examples of the right answer (input → ideal output). RLHF / DPO: preferences between two answers (A is better than B).

They combine: you usually do SFT via LoRA or QLoRA. The lab is exactly that, SFT delivered through QLoRA.

A new framing slide before the details. Students often treat LoRA and SFT as alternatives. They answer different questions: LoRA is how many weights move, SFT is what signal you train on. Every technique on the next slides sits somewhere on these two axes.
