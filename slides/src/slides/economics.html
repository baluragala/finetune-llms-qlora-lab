7 · Economics

Where the money actually goes

Prompting

Free to build

But a recurring cost and latency on every call: long instructions × millions of calls.

RAG

Cheap to keep current

Modest build plus retrieval per call. Update a document, not a model.

Fine-tuning

Pay up front

Training, serving and retraining cost money, but every call is cheaper and faster afterwards.

Fine-tuning trades upfront and maintenance cost for cheaper per-call inference. It pays off at high volume on a stable task, and loses on low-volume or fast-changing ones.

The apprenticeship pays back only if the chef cooks that style thousands of times.

Prompting: free build, recurring per-call cost. RAG: cheap to keep current. Fine-tuning: upfront plus serving plus maintenance, but cheaper per call. In the lab, a 0.5B model with a short prompt replaces a long instruction prompt on a larger model, on every ticket.
